Reflection

This project helped establish the design approach I use today as a Senior Website Designer. By combining features from multiple platforms into one cohesive app, I gained experience thinking through the entire UX/UI process—from early concepts and user needs to interface design and prototyping. Those foundations now inform how I create responsive websites, develop scalable design systems, document reusable components, and collaborate with cross-functional teams throughout discovery, design, development, and launch. Looking back, the project marked an important shift from exploring UX/UI as a career path to building the user-centered, visually clear, and technically practical digital experiences that define my work today.
